# NNNN. Title in the imperative or as a statement of fact

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

What forces this decision. What was true before it, what problem that caused, and which
alternatives were live. Enough that a reader who was not there can reconstruct the
argument without asking anyone.

## Decision

The decision itself, stated flatly and completely enough to be checkable against the code.
If the decision closes a set, list the members. If it fixes a definition, give the
definition and its magnitude.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision — what the schema, the loader and the interface now have to
do, and what a caller can now rely on.

## Cost

What this gives up. Every real decision costs something; a record whose cost section says
"none" is describing a preference, not a decision.

## What would reverse it

The evidence or change in circumstance that would make this the wrong call, and what
unwinding it would take — a schema migration, a re-derivation, an export-boundary change,
or data that cannot be recovered at all.
